FAQs

Do I need to be home?

No — most clients provide a lock code or key. We text when finished.

What about supplies?

We bring everything. If you prefer certain products, tell us!

Do you offer recurring cleanings?

Yes — choose weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly when you request your quote, or book a one-time clean.

How do you handle my keys and entry codes?

We keep them private and use them only for your scheduled visit. Please don’t send codes through the website form — we’ll collect them after you book.

How is my price set?

Prices on this site are estimates based on home size and condition. We confirm your exact quote before your first visit.
